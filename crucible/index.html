Something to think about…how may this play be a hit at today’s government?

Of modern governance by showing how paranoia, overrating power importance, and the abuse of institutional power can corrupt a society. The play's themes show several political dynamics.

This story shows the flaws in human nature and society. Showing the timeless mistakes humans make, for those who forget the past are doomed to repeat it. Loosely based on true, historical events, but heavily fictionalized drama.

Containing witches, a neighbourhood who was full of trust, now turning on themselves, a love affair, and much more within itself.

Comparing intentions and endings (Warning: SPOILERS)

Mary Warren Mary Warren is a timid naive 17-Year-Old girl who works as a servant for John and Elizabeth Proctor Originally Mary WarrenTries to tell the court the truth but with Abigail's intimidating he's in the fusion of John Proctor Witchcraft to save herself. She cracks Under Pressure. She knew deep down that there's no real witchcraft but the fear of Abigail and the court Has her abandoned the truth entirely. Abigail Williams Abigail Williams is a 17-year-old orphan living with her uncle trying to hide her affair with John Proctor and avoiding punishment for dancing in an attempt to summon spirits in the forest with other girls. In act 1 she admits to trying to summon demons and thanks for forgiveness and praise from the adults to have God forgive her. Sometime later she accuses John Proctor Witchcraft of trying to save herself. In her final action she robs her uncle Parris of his life savings to escape and leaves John Proctor to face his own execution.
